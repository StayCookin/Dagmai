Honest constraints

This tool prioritises candidates for lab testing — it does not predict clinical efficacy. It runs on iJO1366 (bundled with cobrapy) rather than iML1515 because this deployment has no network path to the BiGG Models repository; resistance mechanisms are hand-curated rather than pulled live from CARD/RGI against an uploaded genome; and steady-state FBA capacity constraints structurally favor additive/antagonistic Bliss scores over synergy even for textbook synergistic pairs (see README). Non-metabolic drug targets (ribosome, DNA gyrase, RNA polymerase) have no growth-based score at all and fall back to literature notes.
